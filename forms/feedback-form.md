# Google Form: Private Feedback

**Form title:** How can we do better?

**Pre-fill via URL params** (set by landing page before redirect):
- `patient_id` → hidden field
- `rating` → hidden field (pre-populated from landing page star selection)

## Questions

1. **How would you describe your experience today?** *(Paragraph — required)*
2. **What could we have done better?** *(Paragraph — optional)*
3. **Would you like a team member to follow up with you?** *(Multiple choice)*
   - Yes, please call me
   - Yes, please email me
   - No, I just wanted to share feedback

## Confirmation page message

> Thank you for your feedback. We take every comment seriously and are
> committed to making every visit a great experience for your family.
> If you requested a follow-up, our team will be in touch within 1 business day.

## Form settings

- Collect email address: **Off** (reduce friction)
- Limit to 1 response: **Off**
- Show progress bar: **Off**
- Shuffle question order: **Off**
